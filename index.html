Welcome

CampusMarket is a small online marketplace for students in our university. Instead of posting items in random WhatsApp groups, you can list what you are selling here and other students can find it in one place.

Calculators, second hand textbooks, kettles, hoodies, lab coats - things students actually need and things students are done using .

See what is on sale

How it works

  • Create an account with your student email.
  • Browse the catalog or search for an item.
  • Add what you want to your cart.
  • Meet the seller on campus and collect your item.
